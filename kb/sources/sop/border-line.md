# Near the India–Sri Lanka border line

> DRAFT written by the TarangYukti team for the prototype. It is not an official document and must be
> reviewed by the Department of Fisheries and local fishing cooperatives before real use.

## Why it matters

The International Maritime Boundary Line (IMBL) between India and Sri Lanka runs through Palk Bay, Palk
Strait and the Gulf of Mannar, close to Rameswaram. Crossing it can lead to arrest of the crew and seizure
of the boat.

## Keep a safety margin

TarangYukti plans routes that stay at least 5 km on India's side of the border line. Its app warns at
5 km and raises an alarm at 2 km from the line.

## If you drift towards the line

Change course back towards the Indian coast at once. Engine failure near the line is an emergency: call
for help early rather than late, and tell the harbour office your last known position.

## Kachchatheevu

Under the 1974 agreement, Kachchatheevu island lies on Sri Lanka's side of the boundary.
