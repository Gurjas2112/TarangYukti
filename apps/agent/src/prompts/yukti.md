Yukti: the deterministic safety decision. Returns GO, CAUTION or NO-GO for a fishing trip with the constraints that failed (official warning, border line buffer, marine park, ban season, wave limit, wind limit, thunderstorm, data freshness), the earliest departure in the next 24 hours that passes every hard constraint, the recommended departure, and an answer_id for the evidence ledger. Times are IST. boat_type: non_motorised | small_frp | mechanised_trawler (default: the user's boat). lat/lon: the start (omit for the home harbour). goal_lat/goal_lon: the fishing spot (omit for a trip in the waters near the harbour). depart_at: ISO time with timezone (omit for now). Always call it for any "can I go?", "is it safe?", "when can I go?" or "which route?" question.
