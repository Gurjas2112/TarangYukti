Weather Intel: hourly wave height (m), wind and gusts (km/h) for the coming hours at one position, plus every active or upcoming official warning for the region (IMD, INCOIS, state disaster authority). Times are IST. lat/lon: the position (omit for the home harbour, Rameswaram). hours: 1-72 (default 12). Use it for "how are the waves / wind?" and "is there any warning?". It does NOT decide whether to go to sea: use yukti for that.
