# When an official warning is active

> DRAFT written by the TarangYukti team for the prototype. It is not an official document and must be
> reviewed by the Department of Fisheries and local fishing cooperatives before real use.

## Who issues warnings

Official warnings for the sea come from the India Meteorological Department (IMD), the Indian National
Centre for Ocean Information Services (INCOIS) and the State Disaster Management Authority. TarangYukti
only repeats them; it never replaces them.

## Before leaving harbour

If an official high-wave, swell-surge, cyclone, strong-wind or thunderstorm warning covers your fishing
area for any part of the trip, do not go to sea. Wait for the warning to end and check again before
leaving.

## If a warning is issued while you are at sea

Return to the nearest safe harbour as soon as it is safe to do so. Tell your family or the harbour office
that you are returning. Keep life jackets on until you are back.

## After the warning ends

A warning that has ended may be followed by a new one. Check the latest official bulletin before the
next trip; do not rely on a message received earlier in the day.
