# Emergency at sea

> DRAFT written by the TarangYukti team for the prototype. It is not an official document and must be
> reviewed by the Department of Fisheries and local fishing cooperatives before real use.

## Numbers to call

The Indian Coast Guard maritime search and rescue helpline is 1554. The national emergency number is 112.

## Person overboard

Shout "man overboard", keep pointing at the person, and throw anything that floats. Turn the boat back
slowly and approach from downwind. Call for help as soon as you can.

## Engine failure or leaking boat

Put on life jackets first. Drop anchor if the water is shallow enough. Call for help with your boat's
name and your last known position.

## Before every trip

Carry life jackets for everyone on board, a charged phone or radio, and drinking water. Tell your family
where you are going and when you expect to return.
